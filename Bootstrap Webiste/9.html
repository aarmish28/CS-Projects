Technologies

Technology is the application of knowledge for achieving practical goals in a reproducible way.
The word technology can also mean the products resulting from such efforts, including both tangible tools such as utensils or machines, and intangible ones such as software.

Career

Making a strategic decision regarding your future career helps ensure that you'll find satisfaction with your position in the workforce.
As you make your career choice, consider your interests and unique skill set to help you find a career you can succeed in.

Registration

When presented with a form, users may not understand how filing it out and submitting it help them accomplish their goals and tasks.

When designing, clearly indicate benefits of filing out and submitting forms. This is particularly important when users are creating a new account (fie, registration forms), which is the first step before many web applications allow them access to their functionality
